The 1937 Hamilton Bottle

Artifact No. 1 · Glass — Bottles · Unearthed October 25, 2026 · Irish Hill, Martinsburg

Unearthed in Irish Hill, along the Tuscarora Creek in Martinsburg, West Virginia, this small flat-oval bottle is a machine-made druggist’s stock bottle made by the J. T. & A. Hamilton Company of Pittsburgh, to Lawrence J. Kihn’s design — U.S. Design Patent 107,017, patented November 16, 1937. Sold empty and finished with a paper label, it most likely held a toilet preparation — a lotion, hair tonic, or toilet water — from a local drugstore shelf. It was made in 1937 or later, with late-1930s to 1940s production most likely.
