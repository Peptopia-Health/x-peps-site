McCoy Floraline Pedestal Planter

Pottery · Floraline florist ware · Nelson McCoy Pottery Co., Roseville, Ohio · c. 1960s

A tall ribbed pedestal planter in the matte earth tones of McCoy’s Floraline line — florist ware built to be reordered by number and priced for working flower shops. The form and glaze point to the Floraline molds of the 1960s; the molded mark on the base can pin the exact mold and year.

McCoy’s Floraline Strategy

Pottery · Company history · Roseville, Ohio · 1960–1974

How a family ceramics company fought the flood of cheap imported glass: simple forms in solid colors that set off the flowers instead of competing with them, sold directly to florist shops and numbered for easy reordering. Floraline launched on June 9, 1960, and carried McCoy through its final independent decades.
