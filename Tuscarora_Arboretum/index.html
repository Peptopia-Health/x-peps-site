Tuscarora Arboretum

A local-history and archaeology project from Martinsburg, West Virginia — named for Tuscarora Creek, along which the town was laid out in the 1770s. Objects recovered from historic ground are researched exhaustively — patent records, glasshouse catalogs, local history — and published here as full study guides with audio briefings.

The Collection

Artifacts

Objects recovered from historic ground — bottles, marbles, china, and whatever else the Martinsburg soil gives back. Every artifact is researched exhaustively and published with a full study guide and an audio brief.

Explore Artifacts

Plants

The living collection — plants grown, studied, and written up one at a time. Every plant is researched and published with a full guide: its true name, its story, and exactly how to keep it thriving.
